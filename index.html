Free Password Generator — Strong, Random, Secure

PassGen generates cryptographically random passwords using your browser's built-in crypto.getRandomValues() API. Passwords are generated entirely on your device and are never sent to any server, never logged, and never stored. Close the tab and they are gone forever.

What makes a password strong?

  • At least 12 characters long (16+ recommended)
  • Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols
  • Not a dictionary word or personal information (name, DOB)
  • Unique for every account — never reuse passwords

Password vs Passphrase

A passphrase like apple-river-cloud-seven-moon is easier to remember than G#7kLm2@pQ9! and can be just as secure due to its length. Use the Passphrase tab to generate memorable but strong passphrases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to generate passwords online?

PassGen uses crypto.getRandomValues() — the same cryptographic API used by browsers for HTTPS. Passwords are generated locally in your browser and never leave your device. It is safe to use.

How long should a password be?

For most accounts, 16 characters is excellent. For critical accounts (bank, email), use 20+ characters with symbols. Shorter passwords (8–10 chars) are vulnerable to brute force attacks with modern hardware.

Should I use a password manager?

Yes. Use a password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePass) to store unique passwords for every site. PassGen helps you generate those passwords — the manager keeps them safe.

What are the best special characters to include?

Most sites accept: ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) – _ + = [ ] { } | ; : , . ? /. Some systems block certain symbols — if a site rejects your password, try without symbols or use the Alpha+Num option in the Bulk tab.
